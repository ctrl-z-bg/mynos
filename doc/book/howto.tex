\chapter*{HOW TO USE THIS MANUAL}
\markboth{HOW TO USE THIS MANUAL}{HOW TO USE THIS MANUAL}

This manual describes MyNOS in one volume: why it was made, how to use
it, how to program it, and how it works inside.

\textbf{Part I, Introduction}, tells what MyNOS is, why it was begun in
2001, and the systems and ideas of its time.

\textbf{Part II, User's Guide}, is for using MyNOS. It assumes only some
familiarity with a command line. Chapter 13 describes every command, in
alphabetical order.

\textbf{Part III, Programmer's Guide}, is for writing programs for MyNOS,
in OCaml or in C. Chapter 24 holds the reference pages of the libraries,
one page per module.

\textbf{The appendices} explain the inside, for readers who know what a
page table is or want to learn.

The book can be read from the beginning; it can also be consulted. The
contents list every section; the index at the end lists the commands,
the modules and the terms that the text defines.

\section*{TYPOGRAPHIC CONVENTIONS}

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{33mm}@{}p{\dimexpr\linewidth-33mm}@{}}
\texttt{ls /disk} & something you type, or a name in the source tree;\\
\texttt{file.c:123} & a line of the source tree;\\
\texttt{doc/manual} & a path without a slash in front: in the source
tree, the repository \texttt{github.com/ctrl-z-bg/mynos};\\
\texttt{/disk/a.txt} & a path with one: in a running MyNOS;\\
\texttt{mynos-awn} & a bug or a task in the authors' tracker (beads), not
published: \texttt{doc/STATUS.md} and \texttt{doc/JOURNAL.md} use its name;\\
FILE & in a command's syntax, a word you choose;\\
(appendix C.4, p.~1) & a cross-reference: the section, and its page.\\
\end{tabular}

Diagrams are drawn with characters, as they would appear on a terminal.

\begin{mynosfactor}{What these boxes are}
The boxes headed THE MyNOS FACTOR point to what is characteristic of
MyNOS: ideas it takes further than most systems, or does in its own
way. Other systems, past and present, share some of them (chapter 3).
\end{mynosfactor}

The complete programs of Part III are in \texttt{doc/manual/examples} of
the repository, one directory per chapter; the build compiles them all.
